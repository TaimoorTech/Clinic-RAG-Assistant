# Preparing for tests

## Blood tests
Most routine blood tests do not need fasting. A fasting blood test for cholesterol or glucose requires no food or drink except water for 8 to 12 hours beforehand; your request form will say if fasting is needed. Keep taking prescribed medication unless the clinician tells you otherwise. Blood tests are done at the clinic Monday to Friday from 8:00 am to 11:30 am.

## Urine samples
Collect a mid-stream sample in the sterile pot we provide. Hand it in at reception on the same morning, before 11:00 am, with your name and date of birth written on the pot. If you cannot hand it in within a few hours, keep it in the fridge until you do.

## ECG and blood pressure monitoring
An ECG takes about 15 minutes. Wear loose clothing that is easy to remove from the chest area and avoid body lotion on the day. For 24-hour blood pressure monitoring you will wear a small cuff and device; you can go about your normal activities but should avoid showering or swimming during the test.

## Receiving results
Results are normally ready within 3 to 5 working days. You can view them in the patient portal or by calling reception after 2:00 pm. If a result needs action, a clinician will contact you directly.
