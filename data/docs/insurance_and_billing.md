# Fees and insurance

## NHS patients
Registered NHS patients pay nothing for GP consultations, nurse appointments, blood tests and most minor procedures. Prescription charges follow current national rules, and a prepayment certificate can reduce costs if you need many items.

## Private services and fees
Some services are not covered by the NHS and are charged privately, including medical reports for employers or insurers, travel vaccinations not offered on the NHS, and private sick-note letters. A price list is displayed at reception and on the website. We take card payments only; we do not accept cash.

## Private medical insurance
We do not directly bill private medical insurers for GP consultations. We can supply a receipt and a report so you can claim from your insurer yourself. Contact your insurer first to confirm what they cover.

## Registering as a new patient
To register you need proof of address and photo identification. Fill in the registration form at reception or on the website. New patients are offered a health check appointment with the practice nurse within four weeks of registering.
