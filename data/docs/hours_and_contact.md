# Greenfield Family Clinic (fictional sample data)

## Opening hours
The clinic is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 am to 6:00 pm and on Saturday from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm. The clinic is closed on Sundays and on public holidays. The pharmacy counter closes 30 minutes before the clinic.

## Contact details
Phone reception on 020 5550 0142 during opening hours. For non-urgent questions you can email reception@greenfield-clinic.example and we reply within two working days. Our address is 14 Meadow Lane, Greenfield. Free parking is available behind the building, including two accessible bays next to the side entrance.

## Urgent and emergency care
Greenfield Family Clinic does not provide emergency care. In a medical emergency call 999 or go to your nearest emergency department. For urgent but non-emergency advice outside opening hours call 111.
