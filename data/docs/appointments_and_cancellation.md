# Appointments

## How to book
You can book by phone, at the front desk, or online through the patient portal. Same-day urgent appointments are released at 8:00 am each morning and are for new problems that cannot wait. Routine appointments can be booked up to four weeks ahead.

## Cancelling or changing an appointment
Please cancel or change your appointment at least 24 hours in advance by phone or through the patient portal, so that the slot can be offered to someone else. Late cancellations and missed appointments are recorded. After three missed appointments within 12 months we may write to you to discuss how we can help you attend.

## Arriving for your appointment
Please arrive 5 minutes early and bring photo identification on your first visit. If you are more than 10 minutes late we may need to rebook your appointment. Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult unless a clinician has agreed otherwise.

## Telephone and video consultations
Telephone consultations are available for follow-ups and results that do not need an examination. Video consultations need a smartphone or computer with a camera and are booked through the patient portal. Prescriptions can be issued after a telephone consultation when it is clinically safe.
