# Services

## General practice
Our GPs offer consultations for acute illness, long-term condition reviews, medication reviews, referrals, sick notes and general health checks. Standard consultations last 10 minutes. You can request a double appointment of 20 minutes if you have several issues or need an interpreter.

## Diabetes care
We run a diabetes clinic every Tuesday and Thursday morning. It includes annual reviews, HbA1c blood tests, foot checks, retinal screening referrals, and dietary advice from our practice nurse. New patients with a diabetes diagnosis can book a first review with the nurse team through reception.

## Child and family health
We offer childhood vaccinations, baby weight checks and development reviews on Wednesday afternoons. Contraception advice and cervical screening appointments are available with our female GP and nurses on request.

## Minor procedures
Minor procedures include wound dressing, ear syringing, removal of small skin lesions, and joint injections. Some procedures need a prior assessment appointment with a GP, so please do not simply walk in.
